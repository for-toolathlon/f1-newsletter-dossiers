# 2024 British Grand Prix Retro Dossier

**Race:** 2024 British Grand Prix (formally the Formula 1 Qatar Airways British Grand Prix)
**Date:** Sunday, 7 July 2024
**Circuit:** Silverstone Circuit, UK
**Pole:** George Russell (Mercedes) — retired lap 33
**Race winner:** Lewis Hamilton (Mercedes)
**Race position:** 1 · podium: 1. Hamilton (104th career victory; first home win since 2021 Saudi Arabia GP), 2. Verstappen, 3. Norris

## 1. Storyline (official records)
Round: 12 of the 2024 season. Course: permanent racing facility, 5.891 km, total 306.198 km; intermittent rain; attendance 480,000. Pole winner Russell took 1:25.819 in Q3 (107% rule waived on a wet track, so this was valid). During the race Russell fell behind after a water leak mid-race and retired on lap 33, handing Verstappen the drive. Verstappen then won from a 4th-lapped grid position. Storyline: Hamilton wins on his home circuit — his first win since the 2021 Saudi Arabian GP, his record ninth British Grand Prix victory.
